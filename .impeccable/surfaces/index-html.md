# Surface brief: replants.com concept homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. One homepage that stands in for the whole site in a pitch to the owner.

Audience and job: Perth and WA homeowners choosing a grass tree. They need size, price, how it gets home, and how to keep it alive. Action: choose a size and book a pick-up, delivery or installation.

Proof: real Replants photos (salvage at Mahogany Creek, Bruce and the crane truck, Fremantle installs), real price list, real care timeline. No testimonials, no invented awards or counts. Booking, slot availability, SMS reminders and the chat helper are demos and say so on the page.

Constraints: 3D required. Large legible text for an older audience. Works without WebGL and with reduced motion. Pensioner discount percentage is contradictory on the old site: say "ask us", no number. Keep the three helpers working (pick-up slot booking, care reminders, Ask Replants chat).

Revision (user, 2026-10-20, replacement world): "i really dont like the current design… the website doesnt have life. i want a lot of animations". What was wrong: feels like a template, too plain and white, 3D isn't the star, static between moments. Motion wanted: playful and reactive (things answer the cursor and taps, 3D objects you can grab, bouncy transitions). Nothing visual carries over; content and facts only.

Revision (user, 2026-10-20, mid-build): "try to make it as interactive as this https://augrammingwithg.github.io/Plant-shop/?bed=shrubs". That reference is a working shop: a drag-to-turn 3D specimen with prev/next and Add, a tappable stock chart that filters the catalogue and keeps the filter in the URL, filter chips, a header order button with a live count and total, an order drawer with send options, a scroll-lit "how it works" track, drifting foreground leaves, and a chat helper. Match that depth in this world: a real order you build from anywhere, the size chart as tappable pop-up trees kept in the URL, a filterable shelf of everything else Replants sells, the order drawer replacing the inline booking, and the truck riding the delivery track.

Revision (user, 2026-10-20, replacement world, pinned by references): "the design is really ALL OVER THE PLACE. do a full redesign now as quickly as possible". References supplied: a light editorial nature landing (one large tree on a grass island as the hero object, sparkles, scattered small notes, big headline bottom-left, green pill CTA, vertical nav), a centred thin headline with a lime pill highlight and pill nav, a dark forest-green layout with one photo sliced across rounded columns and a numbered process, and photos sliced into staggered rounded pills. The pop-up book world is retired.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One rescued grass tree, presented like a specimen: calm, light, premium. Everything else steps back so the tree and the rescue story carry the page. It refuses both the busy pop-up book and the generic nursery grid.

OWN-WORLD: Bone ground #f4f5ef with faint vertical hairlines and a soft lime glow, ink #131a15, forest #0f2b1d for the one dark chapter and primary buttons, leaf #3b8a4c, lime #cdeb73 used only as pill highlights and active states, sand band #e8e3d4. One typeface: Onest, light 300 for big headlines, 500 for labels, 400 for reading. Pills everywhere: nav, buttons, chips, photo slices. Four-point sparkles as the only ornament.

STORY: See the rescued tree; learn every tree was saved from clearing. Pick a size and see the price. Read the rescue in sliced photos. Add extras, order with pick-up, delivery or install and a real slot. Learn the first two years of care and set reminders. Visit Fremantle or Rockingham, or ask the helper.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light bone canvas framed as one rounded sheet. Centre: a large WebGL grass tree on a floating island of soil and grass, slowly turning, draggable, with drifting motes. Top-left wordmark; right side a vertical nav with an active dash; top-right Ask, phone and order pills. Small notes with sparkles scattered left and right. Bottom-left headline in light Onest with "bulldozer" on a lime pill, and a forest pill CTA "Choose your tree". Bottom-right a glass chip with a round Mahogany Creek photo: "75 trees saved from one block".

FORM: Pinned by user references (no roll this round). Signature: the island tree turns with drag and with scroll, and grows when the size changes. Rescue photos slice into rounded pills that slide into place, staggered. Motion: soft ease-out reveals from visible, pill highlights that wipe in, chips that scale on hover. Reduced motion keeps state changes only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
