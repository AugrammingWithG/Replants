## Users

Perth and regional WA homeowners who want a grass tree (Xanthorrhoea, locally "blackboys") or zamia palm for their garden. They are deciding what size and shape fits their space, what it costs, and whether to collect it themselves, have it delivered, or have it installed. Many arrive with a photo or plan of the spot it will go.

Secondary audience for this concept: the Replants owner, who will judge the mockup as a pitch for replacing replants.com.

## Product Purpose

Replants is a Fremantle enviro-business, founded in 2000 by Bruce Abbott, that salvages grass trees, zamia palms and other native plants from bushland being cleared for development and rehomes them in gardens and landscape projects. The site exists to get a homeowner from "I like grass trees" to a booked pick-up, delivery or installation, with the confidence to keep the tree alive afterwards.

Success for this concept: the client sees a site that sells trees, answers pricing and care questions without a phone call, and makes the rescue story the reason to buy.

## Positioning

Every tree Replants sells was dug out of bush that was about to be bulldozed. Buying one is a rescue, not a nursery purchase. A nursery cannot truthfully claim that.

## Operating Context

- Salvage site: Office Road (off Patterson Road), Rockingham, opposite no. 29. By appointment; pick-ups Saturdays 8am to 12pm per the specials page. Visitors walk into bush to choose trees, which are dug and loaded on their trailer or ute, or tagged for delivery.
- Retail nursery HQ and cultural centre: 96 Wray Avenue, Fremantle 6160. Open Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 4pm. Bagged, nursery-hardened trees (about 20% dearer than salvage-site trees). Call before visiting.
- Three ways to get a tree: DIY pick-up, Delivered (into holes the customer pre-digs, 1m wide x 0.5m deep), Installed.
- Payment: EFTPOS on site at both locations, cash, cheque. Full payment required for delivered or installed trees.
- Regional WA orders by freight (small) or Replants' own crew (large).
- Aftercare: transplant formula, watering by dripper/bubbler (never on foliage), keep mulch 150mm clear of the trunk, remove first flower spike, reapply formula at 6 months. Recovery runs on a 24-month timeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real prices from replants.com (salvage-site trees, by trunk height), to be confirmed by the client before any launch:

- Single head: 0.10m $50, 0.20m $100, 0.40m $150, 0.60m $200, 0.80m $250, 1.00m $300, 1.20m $350
- Multi head: 0.40m $340, 1.20m $750, over 1.20m POA
- Delivery (metro): $100 for one tree, $75 per tree for two or more. Outside metro adds $110/hour travel. Non-standard access $100/hour.
- Installation (to 1.2m trunk): $100 per tree, or $75 if the customer digs. Larger feature trees POA.
- Transplant formula: 0.8kg $15, 1.7kg $25, 15kg $225.
- Pruning: $75 first tree, $25 each after (single head to 1.8m). Optional burning to blacken the trunk, subject to shire rules.
- Maintenance visits: $75 for 1 to 4 trees, $100 for 5 to 10; treatments up to $25 per tree.
- Trailer Special: 3 pre-dug trees (one 0.4m, two 0.2m) plus 3 tubs of formula for $325 (usual $475).
- Warranty: 12 months, only with Replants Transplant Formula used as directed; replacement at 25% of original price plus delivery/install.
- Growth: about 12mm of trunk per year. Sizes range from no-trunk to 4m multi-trunk specimens.
- Other services: small-lot clearing salvage, native garden makeovers, gift vouchers, salvaged native timber (jarrah, marri, tuart, banksia, sheoak).

Open: there is no online ordering today; the current site mentions "place an online order" but has no mechanism. The concept may show an enquiry/booking flow, labelled as a concept.

Known contradictions on the current site to resolve with the client, not silently pick: pensioner discount listed as 10% (pricing page) and 20% (specials page); survival rate "80%" stated against "the past 6 yrs" (stale).

## Product Principles

1. The rescue is the reason. Every page should make clear the tree came from bush that was being cleared.
2. Price and size up front. A homeowner should see what a tree that fits their space costs without phoning.
3. Keep it alive. Aftercare is part of the sale; the warranty depends on it.
4. Real place, real people. Rockingham bush, Fremantle nursery, Bruce and the crane truck, not stock imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews older (pensioner discount, phone-first contact). Text must stay large and readable, the 3D must never hide prices or contact details, and everything must work with reduced motion and without WebGL.
