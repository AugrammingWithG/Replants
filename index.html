    Looking stressed? Four checks
    • New growth in the centre healthy? Brown outer leaves are probably the heat. If not, apply Seasol or Ecovital monthly.
    • Enough water? In summer, 50 L two or three times a week. Once or twice in winter.
    • Too much? The black trunk at ground level should be dry and firm. Pull mulch back if it’s soft.
    • Powder or bugs low in the leaves? Mildew or mealybugs: spray Mancozeb or white oil, not in hot weather.
    12-month warranty

    Plant with Replants Transplant Formula and follow our care guide. If the tree doesn’t make it, we replace it for 25% of the original price, plus delivery and installation if needed. Conditions: drippers not sprinklers, keep the moat, reapply formula at 6 months, remove the first flower spike.
