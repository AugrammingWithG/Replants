      Settling in

      Roots grow fast. Leaf tips, and sometimes the outer leaves, go brown. Don’t worry.

      Fill the moat and soak it. Then deep-water 2–3 times a week in summer.

      • Brown outer leaves are probably the heat. If the centre looks poor, apply Seasol or Ecovital monthly.

      • In summer, 50 L two or three times a week, into the moat. Once or twice in winter. Never on the leaves.

      • The black trunk at ground level should be dry and firm. Pull mulch back if it’s soft.

      • Mildew or mealybugs: spray Mancozeb or white oil, not in hot weather.
